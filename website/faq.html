About CLICK & CO. and who we help

What does CLICK & CO. do?

We design and build websites, and the branding and design material that goes with them, such as logos, social designs, brochures and menus. We also offer marketing consulting.

Who do you work with?

Established and growing businesses that want a professional website that makes it easy for customers to get in touch. Our work so far includes industrial suppliers, logistics, laboratory services and food retail.

Where are you based, and where are your clients?

We are based in Ballito, South Africa. We work with clients in South Africa, the UK and the US.

How much does a website cost?

It depends on the pages, features and design work you need. Tell us about your business and we will send you a clear quote.

What happens after I contact you?

We reply within 24 hours. Then we follow four steps: discover, design, build and launch.

Hosting and care

What is a care plan?

A plan where we host your website and look after it once it is live: updates, backups, fixes and support.

What does a care plan include?

Hosting, updates to your content, regular backups, fixes when something breaks, and support from the team that built your website.

Do I need a care plan?

Every website has to be hosted somewhere and looked after. We recommend a care plan so that someone is responsible for keeping yours online and up to date. Ask us what suits your website.

What if something breaks?

Message us on WhatsApp or email and we will look into it.

What does a care plan cost?

It depends on the website. We include the care plan options in your quote.
